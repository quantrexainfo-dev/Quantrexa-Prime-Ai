We aim to respect user privacy and handle information responsibly. Users should review our privacy policy to understand what data is collected, processed, and stored.

## 🛡️ Security
We continuously aim to improve platform security and protect our services against potential vulnerabilities.

Our AI tools are designed to support learning, creativity, research, and productivity. AI-generated information may contain errors, so users should verify important information.

We aim to communicate our platform's features, limitations, and changes clearly.

We value user feedback and encourage users to report bugs, security concerns, and inappropriate platform behavior through our official support channels.

## ⚖️ Responsible Use
Users are expected to use Quantrexa Prime AI responsibly and legally. Cybersecurity tools should only be used on systems they own or have explicit permission to test.

## 🚀 Our Commitment
Quantrexa Prime AI is committed to improving reliability, transparency, privacy, and user experience as the platform evolves.
