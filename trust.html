Understanding Our Approach

Clear information helps users make informed decisions.

We aim to respect user privacy. For exact information about collected data, retention, sharing and deletion, consult the platform's published Privacy Policy. Do not assume that a feature is private or encrypted unless its implementation explicitly confirms it.
Security is an ongoing responsibility. We aim to identify weaknesses, improve reliability and handle security concerns responsibly. This page does not claim an independent security audit or certification.
No AI system is guaranteed to be error-free. Verify important information using reliable sources, especially for technical, academic, legal or other consequential decisions.
Use security tools only on systems you own or have explicit permission to assess. Follow applicable laws and responsible disclosure practices.

Our Commitment to Users

Quantrexa Prime AI aims to build a useful, transparent and responsible AI workspace. We believe trust requires continuous improvement, honest communication and respect for users.
